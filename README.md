## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a Product Designer who enjoys transforming complex business requirements
into simple, intuitive, and scalable digital experiences.

My work covers the complete product design process—from understanding user
problems and mapping business workflows to building design systems,
high-fidelity interfaces, interactive prototypes, and developer-ready
specifications.

As a Vibe Coder, I use AI-assisted development and agentic workflows to turn
product ideas and UI designs into functional prototypes. This approach helps
me validate concepts faster, understand technical constraints, and collaborate
more effectively with product and engineering teams.

I enjoy working at the intersection of design, technology, and product
strategy—where ideas can be explored, designed, tested, and transformed into
real digital products.

## 🤖 My Agentic Workflow

I use AI agents as collaborative tools throughout the product development
process—from exploring requirements and generating design alternatives to
building prototypes, testing implementations, and automating recurring tasks.

Hermes Agent is part of my agentic workflow for orchestrating tasks,
interacting with tools, processing product information, and supporting
automated workflows across different platforms.

> **Product Idea → Research → User Flow → UI Design → Prototype → AI-Assisted Development → Validation → Iteration**

This workflow helps me move faster without losing focus on usability,
consistency, business goals, and the overall product experience.
