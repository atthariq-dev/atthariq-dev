## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a Product Designer who enjoys transforming complex business requirements
into simple, intuitive, and scalable digital experiences.

My work covers the complete product design process—from understanding user
problems and mapping business flows to building design systems, high-fidelity
interfaces, interactive prototypes, and developer-ready specifications.

As a Vibe Coder, I also use AI-assisted development tools to turn ideas and
designs into functional prototypes. This allows me to validate concepts faster,
understand technical constraints, and collaborate more effectively with
engineering teams.
